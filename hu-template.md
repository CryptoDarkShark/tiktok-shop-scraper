- {{brand_ra}} / {{brand_nak}}: brand name with the correct Hungarian suffix (-ra/-re, -nak/-nek), following vowel harmony and pronunciation. Flag if unsure.
- {{first_name}}: owner/founder first name from Rólunk, Impresszum or LinkedIn, only if clearly stated. Fallback: "Szia {{brand}} csapat!"
- {{product}}: specific product in their currently active Meta ads, in Hungarian, reading naturally before "hirdetéseitek". Fallback: best-known product from homepage.
